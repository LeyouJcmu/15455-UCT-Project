Not easy. Not NP-complete.

If $\P \ne \NP$, then some problem in NP is neither in P nor NP-complete. We prove this in full by building such a problem: SAT padded by an amount that grows just slowly enough to defeat every polynomial-time algorithm, and just quickly enough to destroy NP-completeness.
