Where the two theorems meet.

Mahaney’s Theorem gives a second, more natural route into NPI: every sparse language in $\NP \setminus \P$ is NP-intermediate, if $\P \ne \NP$. In this page, we prove the previous statement, discuss when such languages exist, and survey what happens for more powerful reductions.
