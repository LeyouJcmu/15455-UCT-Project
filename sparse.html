Page 4 / Sparse sets

Every known NP-complete set is dense.

Every NP-complete problem anyone has found turns out to be the same problem in disguise, and all of them are dense. This page explains that observation, the conjecture it led to, and why the conjecture predicts that no sparse set is NP-complete. Mahaney’s Theorem proves that prediction from the weaker assumption $\P \ne \NP$.
