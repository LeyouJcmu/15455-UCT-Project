The full proof.

Given a sparse NP-hard language, we build a polynomial-time algorithm for $\SAT$, using a pruning approach on a walk down the self-reducibility tree, keeping at most $r$ (polynomially bounded) nodes per level. This page proves each lemma from Page 4 and then the theorem.
