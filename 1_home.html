Exploring the classes within NP

If $\P \ne \NP$, then NP is not split cleanly into “easy” problems (P) and “harder” problems (NP-complete): there is a nonempty middle ground, NPI. The following virtual lectures prove that fact (Ladner’s Theorem) and then proves another result, Mahaney’s Theorem: if $\P \neq \NP$, then no sparse set can be NP-hard. Together they show that the structure of NP is even richer than we have previously learned, of course, assuming $\P \neq \NP$.
