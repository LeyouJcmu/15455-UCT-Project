In between $\P$ and $\NP$-complete

If $\P \ne \NP$, then some language in NP is neither in P nor NP-complete. We prove this by artificially constructing such a language: SAT padded by an amount that grows just slowly enough to defeat every polynomial-time algorithm, and just quickly enough to destroy NP-completeness.
