Where the two theorems meet.

Mahaney’s Theorem gives a second, more natural route into NPI: every sparse set in $\NP \setminus \P$ is NP-intermediate, if $\P \ne \NP$. This page proves that, proves Fortune’s theorem with the same left-set machinery, and surveys what happens for more powerful reductions.
