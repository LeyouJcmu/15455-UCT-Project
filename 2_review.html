The tools we assume.

We assume the background of a 455 student: Turing machines, P, NP, polynomial-time reductions, and the Cook–Levin Theorem. This page reviews those and fixes the notation the rest of the site uses. The two genuinely new ideas, sparse sets and the self-reducibility of SAT, are introduced on Page 4, where they are first needed.
